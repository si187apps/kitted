What to wear on Christmas Day

Comfortable enough for a long lunch, smart enough for the in-laws.

By Togged · 24 November 2026 · 2 min read

Christmas Day is a long one: presents in the morning, a big lunch, possibly a walk, then the sofa. Aim for relaxed smart casual: clothes that look like you made an effort for the photos, but that you can sit in for six hours. If you're with the in-laws for the first time, go a notch smarter than you would at home. Plan for a hot house with the oven on all day and a cold walk after lunch.

What to wear, piece by piece

Knitwear
The centre of the outfit. A merino or lambswool crew neck, a shawl-collar cardigan or a fine roll neck in navy, burgundy, forest green, oatmeal or grey. A good wool jumper is around £40–£80; merino breathes better in a warm house than thick acrylic.
Shirt
An Oxford or flannel shirt under the knit, collar out. Plain white, pale blue or a muted check. It means you can take the jumper off when the heating wins and still look put together.
Trousers
Dark jeans with no rips, smart chinos, or wool or cord trousers in brown, navy or olive. Pick a pair with a little room at the waist. You know why.
Shoes
Clean leather or suede boots, loafers or minimal leather trainers for the house, plus waterproof boots by the door for the walk. Bring smart slippers if your hosts are a shoes-off household.
Outer layer
A wool overcoat, a quilted jacket or a waxed jacket for the walk, with a scarf and gloves. Nothing technical needed; something warm you're happy to be photographed in.

What to avoid

  • Thick, heavy jumpers with nothing underneath. You'll be too hot by noon.
  • Tight waistbands and stiff new jeans.
  • Joggers and hoodies if you're a guest, unless your hosts say it's a pyjama day.
  • Light colours you'd hate to get gravy on.

Two ways to wear it

Keep it classic

Navy merino crew neck over a white Oxford shirt, dark jeans, brown suede Chelsea boots and a navy wool overcoat for the walk. Right for any family table.

Push it a little

Burgundy shawl-collar cardigan over a cream roll neck, olive cord trousers, dark brown leather boots and a waxed jacket. Festive without a single reindeer.

Quick questions

Should I wear a Christmas jumper?

If the family does, join in; it's one day a year. Choose a knit pattern like a Fair Isle or a simple snowflake over flashing lights, and wear a plain shirt underneath so you have somewhere to go when it gets warm.

How smart should I go for the in-laws?

One step smarter than the household. A collared shirt under a good knit and proper shoes always reads as respectful, and you can roll your sleeves up later.

Can I do this on a budget?

Yes. One good wool jumper, around £40–£80, does most of the work. Wear it with jeans and boots you already own.
