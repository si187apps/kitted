What to wear on a winter date

Warm outside, sharp inside, and no fighting with a giant coat.

By Togged · 24 November 2026 · 2 min read

A winter date means two climates: a cold walk there, then a warm restaurant, bar or cinema. The trick is layers that look good on their own, so the outfit still works once the coat comes off. Start from our date night guide, then dress for the plan: a little smarter for dinner, a little warmer for a Christmas market, comfy enough to sit for two hours at the cinema.

What to wear, piece by piece

Coat
The piece your date sees first. A wool overcoat in navy, charcoal or camel for dinner or drinks; a quilted jacket, a waxed jacket or a shearling-collared jacket for an outdoor market. A decent wool-blend overcoat is around £80–£200.
Mid layer
A fine merino roll neck, a crew neck over a shirt, a cardigan or an overshirt in wool or cord. Pick one that looks finished on its own: this is what you'll be wearing at the table.
Trousers
Dark jeans with no fading, or wool, flannel or cord trousers in charcoal, brown or navy. Fit matters more than price.
Shoes
Leather or suede Chelsea boots, Derby boots or loafers. For a market or a long walk, leather boots with a grippy sole beat smart shoes on wet cobbles.
Extras
A wool scarf and leather gloves you can take off neatly, a watch and a light touch of fragrance. A beanie in a plain colour is fine for a market; take it off indoors.

What to avoid

  • A bulky puffer to a smart restaurant. It looks out of place and needs somewhere to go.
  • One huge jumper and nothing underneath. You'll overheat inside and can't take it off.
  • Gym trainers on wet pavements; they look tired and soak through.
  • Strong aftershave, which gets stronger in a warm room.

Two ways to wear it

Dinner or drinks

Charcoal wool overcoat, black merino roll neck, grey flannel trousers and black suede Chelsea boots. Sharp at the table, warm on the walk home.

Market or cinema

Navy quilted jacket over a cream crew neck and a blue Oxford shirt, dark jeans, brown leather boots with a grippy sole and a grey wool scarf.

Quick questions

Is a roll neck too much?

No. A fine roll neck is one of the easiest winter date pieces: smart under a coat or jacket, relaxed on its own. Choose a thin merino so it doesn't swamp you.

What if we're outside all evening?

For a Christmas market or ice rink, layer a thin merino or thermal top under a shirt or knit, wear boots with thick wool socks, and pick a coat with a hood or bring a hat. Warm and comfortable beats sharp and shivering.

Can I do this on a budget?

Spend on the coat first, since it does the most work, and wear it with jeans and boots you already own. A plain merino roll neck at around £30–£60 is the next best buy.
