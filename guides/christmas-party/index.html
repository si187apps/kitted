What to wear to a Christmas party

Festive and smart, without looking like you're in fancy dress.

By Togged · 10 November 2026 · 2 min read

Most Christmas parties, work ones especially, sit at smart casual: smarter than the office on a Friday, short of a suit and tie. Check the invite first. If it says black tie or cocktail, read those guides instead. Otherwise, aim for dark, well-fitting clothes with one festive touch, and shoes you can stand in all night.

What to wear, piece by piece

Jacket
A blazer or an unstructured jacket in navy, charcoal or a deep colour like bottle green. For a relaxed party, a good overshirt worn open does the job.
Top
A crisp shirt, a fine merino roll neck or a smart knit. A roll neck under a blazer looks sharp with no tie needed.
Trousers
Dark wool trousers or smart chinos that fit well. Dark jeans only if the party is relaxed and the rest of the look is smart.
Shoes
Polished leather shoes or boots: Chelsea boots, Derbies or loafers. You will be standing for hours, so pick a pair you have already worn in.
Extras
One festive touch at most: velvet, burgundy, bottle green or a subtle pattern. A watch and a belt that matches your shoes.

What to avoid

  • Novelty jumpers, unless the invite asks for them.
  • Gym trainers and sports tops.
  • More than one statement piece at once.
  • Anything you wouldn't want in the photos. They last longer than the party.

Two ways to wear it

Keep it classic

Navy blazer over a white shirt, charcoal wool trousers and black polished Chelsea boots. Smart, safe and right for almost any office party.

Push it a little

Bottle green velvet jacket over a black merino roll neck, black wool trousers and black loafers. Festive without trying too hard.

Quick questions

Is a suit too much?

For most work parties, a suit without a tie is fine, but a blazer and odd trousers feel more relaxed. Wear the suit if the venue is very smart or the invite says business or lounge suit.

Should I wear a Christmas jumper?

Only if the invite says so, or it is a deliberately casual party. Otherwise a deep-coloured knit gives the festive feel without the novelty.
