The fine print, up front

Straight with you.

No subscription

Your looks are free, and so is your first AI preview. The optional Occasion pack is a one-off payment through Apple for 8 more previews.

Your answers, handled with care

No account. Your answers are sent to Google Gemini, an AI service, to build your outfits; we don't store them. If you use See it on you, your photo is used only to make the preview and isn't stored. Privacy notice.

Free to use

Menswear, 18 and over. Your looks are free; the Occasion pack for extra AI previews is optional.
