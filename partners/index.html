What Kitted is

Occasion in.
Complete outfit out.

A shopper tells Kitted where he's going and what he wants to spend. Instead of browsing single items, he gets whole outfits he can buy, priced against his budget.

Alongside the app we publish free, plain-English style guides for occasions and dress codes, from wedding guest to black tie.

01

The plan

The occasion, the place, the dates and a total budget.

02

Parts of the trip

Kitted splits the trip into the moments that need different clothes, for example daytime, evening and hospitality.

03

Priced outfits

Complete outfits for each part, built from retailer product feeds and totalled against the budget. Any piece can be swapped.

04

Pack and share

A packing list of every piece across the chosen looks, and a way to share a look with someone before buying.

How your products appear

Your feed. Your prices.
Your product pages.

Kitted only shows a retailer's products once we're approved for their programme, and only from the official affiliate product feed.

Official feed only

Products come from your affiliate product feed, with their real product IDs, prices and links. We don't scrape your site.

Prices from the feed

AI helps choose and combine pieces. Every price and outfit total is recomputed from the feed, never taken from the AI.

Your own product page

Each shop link opens your product page in the phone's browser, so standard network tracking applies and the sale happens on your site.

"Ad" before every link

Every shop link is labelled "Ad", and we explain that we may earn commission. Shoppers know what they're tapping.

Honest about postage

When an outfit mixes shops, Kitted notes that pieces arrive separately and each retailer's delivery charges may apply.

Images as supplied

Product images are shown as they come in your feed. We don't edit them without your written permission.

What we don't do

Clean traffic.
No tricks.

Kitted earns its clicks inside the app. Shoppers reach you because an outfit they chose includes your product.

  • No paid searchWe don't run paid search campaigns.
  • No brand biddingWe never bid on your brand name, trademarks or variations of them.
  • No voucher codesWe don't publish or promote discount codes.
  • No cashbackWe don't offer cashback or rewards for buying.
  • No edited product imagesWe don't alter your product images without your written permission.
Who it's for

Men with somewhere
to be.

UK men aged 18 and over. Many aren't confident about clothes and want to be told what works for a specific occasion, rather than browse.

They come with a real reason to buy: a wedding, a race weekend, a work event, a trip away. They can optionally give an age band, and choose between "keep it classic" and "push me a little", so the suggestions match how far they want to go.
